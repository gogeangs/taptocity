## Exact prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Nine atmospheric background illustrations for a Korean indie comic post-apocalyptic survival game, EXACT 3 columns x 3 rows, all panels equal width and height, clean hard boundaries without borders or gutters. Match the reference's hand ink crosshatching and muted gouache, warm ivory worn surfaces, slate shadows, tiny amber lights, quiet human drama rather than photorealism. No people, no monsters, no writing, no captions or UI. Row1: ruined Korean shopping alley with broken storefronts and scattered timber; abandoned elevated highway with motionless cars fading into ash fog; dark apartment towers across a river with one small warm window. Row2: old power plant exterior with smokestacks in thick ash haze; abandoned subway platform with rails and a single hanging lamp; damp brick sewer passage with moss and shallow reflecting water. Row3: underground concrete civil-defense bunker corridor with heavy steel doors and supply crates; old power plant underground control room with analog consoles and faint amber indicator lights; small survivor settlement at dusk with patched tent, a lantern and humble wooden workshop, warm hopeful gathering place but no people visible. Each panel is a cinematic establishing shot with clear depth, strong shapes, economical details. Light must be readable even when cropped into a shallow game header. Nine separate distinct compositions, consistent world. Opaque full-bleed atlas.
