## Exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Transparent combat sprite atlas for the same Korean indie comic post-disaster survival game as the attached reference. Four isolated ink-and-gouache ASH CREATURES in exact 2 columns x 2 rows. Top left: regular thin ash creature, a hunched charcoal silhouette of swirling ash and torn fabric-like smoke, two small ember red eyes, wispy feet, clear narrow shoulders, three-quarter view facing slightly left. Top right: SAME regular creature leaning forward to attack. Bottom left: large brute ash creature, broad stone-like shoulders and thick forearms made of compacted ash, squat powerful silhouette, two small ember eyes. Bottom right: SAME brute leaning forward to attack. Nonhuman supernatural enemies made of ash, no gore, no blood, no detailed human flesh, no weapons. Quiet slate and charcoal forms with pale edge highlights so they remain visible on a dark slate map. Hand ink contour, coarse gouache shading, visible brush marks, no glossy 3D. Generous transparent gutters, same baseline and size within each pair, isolated with no scenery or labels, compact wispy shadows only. True transparent alpha background.
