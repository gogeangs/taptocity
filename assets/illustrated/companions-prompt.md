# Production atlas

Built-in image generation, using exploration-sites.webp as style reference. Transparent PNG encoded to WebP quality 90 without retouching. Runtime frame rectangles use measured alpha bounds.

## Exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Transparent sprite atlas for an isometric Korean post-disaster survival game. Match the reference's expressive hand ink contour, muted gouache shading, warm worn colors, clear silhouette; draw ANIMALS rather than buildings. EXACT 4 columns x 3 rows in equal cells with wide transparent gutters; last bottom right cell empty. ELEVEN full body animal companions, one per cell. All animals standing in a relaxed natural pose on invisible ground, three quarter side view FACING RIGHT, legs paws ears and tail fully visible. Not chibi, no huge eyes, no clothes, NO collars or accessories (these are drawn dynamically in game). Row1: friendly tan floppy-eared mixed breed dog; ash gray striped cat with raised tail; charcoal crow; cream-white small horned goat. Row2: brown hedgehog; silver white fox with flowing pale tail; old mossy olive turtle; brown tufted owl. Row3: sandy fennec fox with very large ears; handmade scrap-metal robot dog with small cyan eye and jointed legs; Asian black bear cub with white crescent chest marking; blank. Consistent brush and ink style, controlled detail that works at small mobile sprite size and in a 120px companion portrait. No text, labels, scene, props, floor patches or cast shadows, isolated animals on true transparent background. Keep anatomical silhouettes distinct and beautiful. Entire bodies fit within each equal cell.
